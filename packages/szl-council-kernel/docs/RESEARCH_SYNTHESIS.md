# Research synthesis: borrow the mechanisms, own the governance layer

The Research Foundry catalog is discovery metadata, not vendored code. Every item remains `DISCOVERED` until license, revision, safety, reproduction, benchmark, design review, and Fourfold promotion close.

## What to borrow

- **Temporal:** durable workflow history, replay, Activities, retries, and recovery. Keep Council authority outside workflow framework internals.
- **LangGraph:** fine-grained deterministic/agentic state graphs and human checkpoints. Do not use chat state as authoritative proof.
- **OPA/Rego:** externalized policy evaluation with explicit inputs and decisions.
- **SPIFFE/SPIRE:** workload identity, short-lived SVIDs, and trust-domain accounting.
- **MCP:** interoperable agent-to-tool access, always behind the MCP Governor.
- **A2A:** opaque cross-framework agent interoperability, always behind identity and policy binding.
- **SCITT and transparency logs:** content-agnostic signed statements, receipts, inclusion, consistency, and monitor verification.
- **in-toto/SLSA/Sigstore:** build/deployment provenance and identity-linked artifact signing.
- **Mixture-of-Agents research:** use strong single-model and heterogeneous ensembles as measured baselines; do not equate different endpoint names with independent evidence.
- **Conformal risk control research:** calibrate the action trajectory and high-risk semantic argument roles, not only the final natural-language answer.
- **Contract-grounded tool execution:** model tool calls should compile into preconditions, postconditions, and symbolic state transitions.

## Novelty discipline

Do not claim an idea is unique merely because the labels are new. Before publication, run a literature, standards, patent, and product comparison; state novelty narrowly; reproduce upstream baselines; and publish negative results.
