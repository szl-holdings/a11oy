The thesis

Governed AI you can prove — not AI you're asked to trust.

Most AI asks for trust. a11oy earns it the way auditable systems do: every governed state change is recorded in a hash-chained receipt, while signer state and signature verification are disclosed separately and never inferred; a trust gate can only tighten an answer, never wave it through; and the kernel rests on machine-checked theorems you can read. The honest part is the product — when a claim isn't proven, we label it, and you can open the check yourself.
