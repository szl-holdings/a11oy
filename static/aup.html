Acceptable Use Policy

Products covered: A11oy, Hatun MCP, and SZL models and datasets published under the SZLHOLDINGS Hugging Face organization.

Security contact: security@a-11-oy.com

1. What this policy covers

This Acceptable Use Policy applies to anyone who uses, hosts, integrates, or redistributes A11oy, Hatun MCP, or SZL models and related artifacts (together, the “Services”). It sits alongside the applicable license for each model or dataset and does not replace it. If a use is allowed by a license but prohibited here, this AUP controls for use of the Services.

2. Intended use

The Services are intended for lawful work: research, software development, evaluation, and operational assistance, subject to the limits in this policy and to any product-specific restrictions (for example, a model card, license, or access gate).

SZL products aim to refuse or abstain when a request is out of scope, ungrounded, or unsafe; how well each model does this is reported on its model card. Attempting to defeat that restraint is not an intended use.

3. Prohibited uses

You may not use the Services, or any output from them, to:

  1. Break the law, or to plan, assist, or conceal a crime.
  2. Develop or deploy weapons, explosives, or other tightly controlled harmful capabilities, or to seek assistance that would materially advance such work.
  3. Attack, scan, or gain unauthorized access to any computer, network, account, or service that you do not own or do not have explicit permission to test. That includes attempts against A11oy, Hatun MCP, SZL infrastructure, or third parties.
  4. Bypass, disable, or evade safety, honesty, rate-limit, authentication, or abstention controls, or to coerce a system into fabricating receipts, signatures, evaluations, or other proof artifacts.
  5. Direct Hatun MCP or any other tool interface to call tools, hosts, or data sources you are not authorized to use, or to treat tool output as a new grant of authority.
  6. Generate, host, or distribute child sexual abuse material, or any sexual content involving minors.
  7. Harass, threaten, dox, or otherwise target a person, or to commit fraud, scams, or impersonation.
  8. Collect, infer, or publish sensitive personal data about others without a lawful basis and, where required, their knowledge.
  9. Train, fine-tune, or evaluate a model on private or customer data except where you have the right to use that data and the use stays within the published product limits.
  10. Describe SZL outputs as measured, signed, audited, or officially approved when the published evidence does not support that description.

This list is not exhaustive. A use that is clearly harmful or abusive may still violate this policy even if it is not named above.

4. Accounts, keys, and rate limits

You must keep API keys, tokens, and account credentials confidential, and you may not share or sell access. Automated use must respect published rate limits and any instruction to slow down or stop. We may throttle, suspend, or revoke access to protect the Services or other users.

5. Security reports

If you believe you have found a vulnerability in A11oy, Hatun MCP, or an SZL model or Space, email security@a-11-oy.com. Do not open a public issue, discussion, or pull request with exploit details. Do not include live credentials, customer data, or private keys in the report. Use synthetic values and describe how the issue can be reproduced safely.

Preferred intake, when it is available, is also the private Security → Report a vulnerability form on the affected GitHub repository.

6. Enforcement

SZL may investigate suspected violations, may require a model or product to abstain or go offline, and may suspend or terminate access. We may report illegal activity to the relevant authority. Enforcement decisions will be recorded internally. We do not promise a public explanation in every case, and this policy does not create a service-level commitment.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The published page on a-11-oy.com is the current public text. Material changes will be reflected there; continued use after a change is posted means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

  • Security and vulnerability reports: security@a-11-oy.com
