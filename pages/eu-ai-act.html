EU AI Act Article 12: a technical logging map.

Article 12 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 describes logging capabilities for high-risk AI systems. This page maps selected provisions to fields in a self-authored GovernedAction/v1 conformance profile. It does not determine whether a system is high-risk, whether a deployment records and retains the required logs, or whether that system complies with the Act.

Application dates in the consolidated text

As of 7 October 2026, Article 113 of the consolidated Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 schedules Chapter III, Sections 1–3, including Article 12, to apply from 2 December 2027 for Annex III high-risk systems and 2 August 2028 for Annex I high-risk systems. Articles 19(1) and 26(6) describe a minimum six-month retention period for logs under the provider's or deployer's control, subject to applicable Union or national law. The European Commission's AI Act timeline reflects those dates.

What the declared profile checks

The source profile names selected fields and structural validators. A passing structural check would establish only that the supplied artifact has those fields. It would not prove that a deployed system automatically logs every relevant event or retains the logs. The specific minimum fields in Article 12(3) apply to the biometric systems described in Annex III, point 1(a), rather than every high-risk system.

Evidence boundary

DECLARED The mapping is recorded in the profile and its demo harness. UNKNOWN This page has no deployment-specific readback of automatic capture, durable retention, signing-key trust, or external verification. Those require separate evidence for the system and workflow being assessed.

Scope

  • This technical map is not legal advice. Whether a particular system is in scope, and which obligations apply, needs a deployment-specific assessment.
  • The profile is a self-assessment artifact, not an EU conformity assessment or certification.
  • A receipt or verifier result can support an integrity review only after its key, issuer, payload, and storage boundary are independently established. It does not establish that the recorded event is factually correct.
